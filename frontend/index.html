How MealFinder Works

1

Set Your Macros

Enter your height, weight, and goals. We automatically calculate your exact daily calorie and protein targets using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation.

2

Generate & Discover

Let our Monte Carlo simulation generate full daily meal combos that hit your exact targets, or manually search for specific cravings.

3

Track & Shop

Log your meals to hit your daily tracker, and push entire combos to the Groceries tab to instantly aggregate your shopping list with estimated pricing.
